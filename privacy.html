Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

DERMAKRAFT ("we", "us", "our") operates a curated skincare marketplace connecting independent sellers with customers across Nepal. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and order details. If you sign up as a seller, we additionally collect your business name, contact details, and product listings you choose to publish.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To send order confirmations and tracking updates via email
  • To verify payments made through eSewa or Khalti
  • To communicate with sellers about orders containing their products
  • To improve our platform and customer experience

3. Sharing With Sellers

When you order a product from a specific seller on our platform, we share the necessary order details (item, quantity, delivery location) with that seller so they can fulfil your order. We do not share your full contact details beyond what's needed for delivery.

4. Payment Information

DERMAKRAFT does not store your eSewa or Khalti account credentials or card details. Payments are verified manually against the transaction reference you provide; we do not process or hold funds on your device or browser.

5. Data Storage

Order and account data is stored securely using Supabase, our database provider. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no online system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email below. Sellers may update or remove their listings at any time from their dashboard.

7. Cookies

We use minimal local browser storage to remember your cart contents between visits. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of DERMAKRAFT after changes means you accept the revised policy.
